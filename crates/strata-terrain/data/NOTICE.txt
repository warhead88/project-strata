Project Strata prepared Earth surface

Relief and bathymetry: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information
(2022). ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model, version 1, distributed
60 arc-second ice-surface GeoTIFF variant. DOI: 10.25921/fd45-gt74.
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/etopo-global-relief-model
The NOAA user guide permits private, academic, and commercial use.
https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/mgg/global/relief/ETOPO2022/docs/1.2%20ETOPO%202022%20User%20Guide.pdf

Land, islands, lakes, river/canal centrelines and marine names: Made with
Natural Earth. 1:10m physical vectors. Public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Project Strata derived the integer elevation grids, sampled water levels,
synthetic channel beds and bank/valley transitions, and the sparse water
atlas from these sources. See tools/earth/sources.sha256 for exact inputs.
